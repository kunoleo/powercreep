AI content

Links, not uploads

Creators paste a link or connect an account. Power Creep stores the link and shows the platform's own player, so the platform hosts and moderates the file.

Two AI labels

"AI-assisted" means made by the person with AI helping somewhere. "AI-made" means mostly or fully generated. Creators add a short note explaining what was AI.

Reports go to staff

Each report lands in the staff inbox sorted by category. Illegal-content reports always sit at the top.

Messages

End-to-end encrypted

Messages are locked on the sender's device and unlocked only on the receiver's. Power Creep's servers and staff only ever hold scrambled text.

So how are DMs moderated?

Staff can't read them. When someone reports a conversation, their own app decrypts the last messages and sends that copy with the report, the same approach WhatsApp and Messenger use.

Spam protection

Without reading messages, staff can still use rate limits, report counts and "only people I follow can DM me" settings.
