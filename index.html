Terms of Use

Last updated 29 October 2026

The short version: Be decent. Only post your own work. Label anything made with AI. Don't break the law. You're responsible for what you post, link and send.

Power Creep is a free place to gather the work you've posted all over the internet. By making an account or using the site, you agree to these terms. They're written to be read, so please do.

1. Who can use Power Creep

  • You must be at least 13 years old. Some countries set a higher age, up to 16. If yours does, you need to be that age or have permission from a parent.
  • One person per account. Don't make new accounts to get around a ban.
  • Keep your password safe. You're responsible for what happens on your account. If you think someone else got into it, change your password and contact us.

2. Your work stays yours

You keep all rights to what you post. By posting, you give Power Creep permission to store and show it on the site, for as long as it's up. That permission ends when you delete it, except where the law requires us to keep something for a while.

Games, videos, music and images you link stay on the sites where they're hosted, like YouTube, SoundCloud or itch.io. Those sites' own terms apply to them.

3. Rules

These aren't allowed anywhere on Power Creep, including profiles, posts, comments, links and messages:

  • Nudity or sexual content.
  • Anything sexual involving minors. Zero tolerance. We remove it, ban the account and report it to the police.
  • Illegal content, like pirated games, stolen accounts or data, or selling illegal things.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate against people for who they are.
  • Sharing someone's private information, like their address, phone number or real name, without their permission.
  • Real gore or shock content posted to upset people. Horror games and art are welcome. Real violence isn't.
  • Encouraging self-harm or dangerous acts.
  • Pretending to be someone else, or posting other people's work as your own.
  • Spam, scams and harmful links, including phishing, malware and misleading buttons.
  • Hiding AI use. Mark work as AI-assisted or AI-made when it is, with a short note saying what was AI.
  • Attacking the site, like hacking, mass-copying content with bots, overloading the servers, or getting around a ban.

4. You're responsible for what you do here

You're responsible for everything you post, link and send, and for following the law where you live. If you break the law using Power Creep, you are the one legally responsible for it, not Power Creep.

We don't check everything before it goes up. If the police or a court sends a valid legal order, we have to hand over the information we have about the account involved. By design, that's very little.

If something you posted gets Power Creep into a legal dispute, you may have to cover the costs, as far as the law allows.

5. Reports and moderation

  • Anyone can report a profile, post, comment, piece of media or chat with the Report button. The person you report never finds out who reported them.
  • Staff review reports. Depending on how serious it is, we may remove content, add an AI label, warn the account, or suspend or ban it.
  • If we remove something of yours, we tell you what and why, unless telling you would put someone in danger or break the law.
  • If you think we got it wrong, you can appeal within 6 months by contacting us. A person will look at it again.
  • To report illegal content without an account, email kunoleo@gmail.com with a link to it and why you think it's illegal.
  • If someone posted your work as theirs, report it as "Not their content" and link to your original.

6. Links and embedded players

Power Creep doesn't host files. It shows links to other sites, and their players when you press play. We're not responsible for other sites. Their own terms and privacy policies apply when you use them.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time under Edit profile. This removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, follows and links. We may close accounts that seriously or repeatedly break the rules.

8. The service

Power Creep is free and provided as it is. We may change, pause or shut it down, and we'll try to give notice first. We aren't responsible for losses from using the site, except where the law doesn't allow us to limit that. Nothing here takes away your rights as a consumer.

9. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we'll update this page. For important changes, we'll tell you on the site before they take effect. If you don't agree, you can delete your account.

10. Contact

Power Creep is run by Kuno Leo. Email: kunoleo@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

The short version: We store as little as we can. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data. You can download or delete everything at any time.

1. Who's responsible for your data

Power Creep is run by Kuno Leo, Sweden, who is responsible for your data (the "data controller" under the EU's GDPR). Questions or requests: kunoleo@gmail.com

The For you feed is worked out from your own likes and comments. We don't track what you look at, how long you look at it, or what you click.

3. What we never collect

  • Your real name, birthday, phone number, location or contacts, unless you choose to write them on your profile.
  • Analytics, tracking pixels or advertising profiles.
  • Anything to sell. We never sell or share your data for marketing.

4. Other companies involved

  • Supabase runs our database and login system, on servers in the EU (Ireland), under a data processing agreement with us.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website's files. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google, only if you choose "Sign up with Google" (not switched on yet). Google confirms who you are and gives us your email address. Google knows you used it.
  • Gravatar, only if you choose a Gravatar avatar. A scrambled code made from your email is used to load the picture.
  • YouTube, SoundCloud, Twitch, Spotify, Vimeo and Bandcamp. Their players only load when you press play, and from then on their own privacy policies apply. YouTube videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Thumbnails and linked images load straight from the sites that host them, like YouTube or Imgur. Like any picture on the web, that means those sites can see your IP address when the picture loads.

Some of these companies are based outside the EU, mainly in the US. They're covered by safeguards approved by the EU for this kind of transfer.

5. How long we keep things

  • Your account data, until you delete it. Deleting your account removes everything from the site straight away, and from backups within 30 days.
  • Things you delete yourself, like a post, are removed the same way.
  • Reports, until 6 months after they're closed.
  • Security logs, for as long as our providers keep them, usually days or weeks.

6. Your rights

  • See and download your data: use Download my data under Edit profile.
  • Fix it: change anything under Edit profile.
  • Delete it: use Delete my account under Edit profile.
  • Object or ask us to limit how we use your data, by emailing us.
  • Complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in the country where you live. In Sweden, that's IMY (imy.se).

We answer requests within one month.

7. Children

Power Creep is for people aged 13 and up, or older where local law requires it. If we learn that a younger child has made an account, we delete it.

8. Keeping data safe

The site only works over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, messages are end-to-end encrypted, and only staff who need it can see reports.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and, for important changes, tell you on the site first.

Cookie Policy

The short version: Power Creep doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. It only saves what it needs to keep you logged in and remember your settings, which is why there's no cookie banner.

Other sites' cookies

  • Players from YouTube, SoundCloud, Twitch, Spotify, Vimeo and Bandcamp only load when you press play. After that, those sites may set their own cookies. Their cookie policies apply.
  • Google sign-in and Gravatar only come into play if you choose to use them.

Clearing them

Logging out removes your login. You can clear everything Power Creep saved through your browser's settings, under site data or cookies.
